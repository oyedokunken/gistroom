# Security policy

## Supported versions

Gistroom is pre release. Only the `main` branch is supported. From v1.0.0 the latest release is supported.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email security@gistroom.ng with:

- what you found and where,
- steps to reproduce it,
- the impact you expect.

Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.

Response times:

| Step                                | Target         |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Acknowledge your report             | 3 working days |
| First assessment                    | 7 working days |
| Fix or mitigation for high severity | 30 days        |

## Scope

In scope: `gistroom.ng`, the web app, the realtime service and their APIs.

Out of scope: denial of service by volume, social engineering of staff or users, spam, findings that need a rooted or jailbroken device, and third party services (Supabase, LiveKit, Vercel, Render, Google).

## Safe harbor

If you act in good faith, stay inside the scope above, avoid reading or changing other people's data beyond what proves the issue, and give us reasonable time to fix it before you disclose, we will not pursue legal action against you.

## Abuse and takedown

To report abusive media or content, use the report control in the app or email safety@gistroom.ng. Reports on messages capture the last 20 messages as evidence. We remove content that breaks the Acceptable Use rules and record the action in the audit log.
