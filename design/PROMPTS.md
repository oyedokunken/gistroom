# Image prompts

Paste the style lock first, then the asset prompt. Save masters in `design/masters/` (the optimizer script arrives with the first real art).
Never let an image generator draw letters. Text is composed in code with Graphik (CLAUDE.md Section 17.4).

## Style lock

Contemporary African editorial illustration. Warm, confident, human. Flat shapes with soft grain and gentle shading, bold simple forms, one consistent light source from the upper left. Palette locked to green (#0E7A43, #0A5C32, #E4F4EB), yellow (#FFC20E, #FFF4CC), near black (#0B0B0C), white and greys, plus natural skin tones and small warm accents. Characters are Nigerian and varied: different ages, body types, skin tones, hair and head wraps, agbada, ankara, hijab, caps, casual wear. Settings are real gist spaces: a rooftop, a joint under an umbrella, a barbershop, a living room, a campus step, a danfo bus, a football viewing centre, lantern light at night. Show energy through gesture and speaking rings, not caricature. NO text, letters or numbers anywhere in the image. Never: stock photo office scenes, poverty or safari tropes, caricature, real people's likeness, trademarks, the national flag or coat of arms, sacred or ritual symbols, emoji.
