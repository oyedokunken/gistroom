# SEO

Status: Home page only so far. Public pages arrive in Phase 6.

The public site must rank and share well. The app shell is private.

- Home has a unique title and description, canonical URL, Open Graph and Twitter tags, `lang` set to `en-NG`, and Organization and WebSite JSON-LD.
- Every public page needs exactly one h1, alt text from `content/alt.ts`, and metadata checked by tests.
- Authed pages are `noindex`. `robots` and `sitemap` routes are not written yet.
